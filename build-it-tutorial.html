The 7-step build (ChatGPT — works on free)

1

Open your tool

Go to chatgpt.com and log in. Free account is fine — no upgrade needed for this.

2

Start a new chat

Click New chat (top-left). This is now your agent's workspace.

3

Paste your agent prompt as the FIRST message

Copy the prompt from your Battle-Tested Card, paste it, and press send. That single message programs the agent — everything after, it works for you.

You are my referral & closing agent. I run a home-services business and my superpower is turning skeptical homeowners into signed contracts. I'll give you my past customers' names and past inquiries. For each customer, write a warm 3-line text asking if one worried friend needs my help that I'll personally take care of. For each old inquiry, write a 5-line follow-up with one opening. Keep it human. Ask me for my service, my price, and names first.
4

Save it as your agent

Click the chat's title and rename it (e.g., "My Closing Agent"). On the sidebar, pin the chat so it stays at the top. That's your agent, saved and always one click away.

5

Test it on ONE real scenario

Give it a real customer or a real objection you faced this week. Check: is it human? Is it "you"? Ask it to make it warmer, shorter, less salesy — it tunes to your voice.

6

Day 2 — feed it your business

Paste your real offers, your real prices, the phrases only you say. The more you feed it, the more it writes like you.

7

Day 3 — make it a habit

Use the pinned chat every day: this week's referrals, the follow-ups, the tough replies. Within a week it's a worker you trust.

Build your agent on ChatGPT — you already have it

Every avenue below becomes a saved ChatGPT agent you build in about 15 minutes — no new signups, free, on the tool you already use. Once you see it work, a free connector can make it actually send and write on its own. The goal here is to prove the capacity — and it does.

How it works on ChatGPT (free)

  1. Open chatgpt.com (free). Click New chat (or create a Project to keep it organized).
  2. Paste the avenue's instructions below as your first message and send — that single message programs the agent.
  3. Test it with one real example (a real customer, a real invoice, one idea). It performs the workflow instantly.
  4. Pin / name the chat so it stays at the top. That's your saved agent — it remembers you across the conversation.
1 · Email & follow-ups most common›
The pain

Warm leads and past customers go quiet and you never chase them — you lose deals you already earned.

The ChatGPT agent

You give it your list of missed leads / past customers. It writes a personal, human follow-up for each — ready to send or copy.

Build it (5 min)

New chat → paste these instructions → paste 10 customer names + notes → it returns 10 warm follow-ups.

You are my follow-up agent. For each person I give you — name, when I last talked, one note — write a short, warm, 3-line message that reconnects and offers ONE specific next step. No hard sell, no spam feel, sound like a real person.
Make it act (optional): connect Gmail + Google Sheets through a free connector (Zapier) so the follow-ups send automatically on a schedule.

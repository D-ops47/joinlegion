3 · How they work inside ChatGPT

Three layers — the brain, the connection, the test:

🧠 The brain (ChatGPT): each lesson hands them the exact prompt to paste into a ChatGPT chat/Project. They build and talk to their agent there — it thinks, writes, and understands their business.

🔗 The connection (free connector): to make it act — send the email, write the sheet row, ping Slack — we walk them through a free connector (Zapier) connected to their real Gmail/Sheets/Slack/Monday/Outlook. Copy-paste the Zap's AI instruction; they click it into place.

✅ The test (their business): the course says "now test it on one real customer / one real invoice / one real idea." They run it, see it work, and tweak it with our guidance.

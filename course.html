DAY1
See it — what an AI agent actually is
~15 min · feel it, don't just read about it
›
Learn

An AI agent isn't a chatbot you talk to — it's a worker you train. Chatbots answer questions; agents take a task and get it done, using your instructions and your information. The good news: the tool you already have — ChatGPT — is where agents are born. No new signups, no software to install.

Do it in ChatGPT (2 minutes)

Open chatgpt.com → New chat → paste this and send:

Act as my superpower assistant. Ask me, one question at a time, about the ONE task that eats the most of my time. Then show me 3 clear ways you could help me with it — keep it short, practical, and human.

Answer its questions. Watch it come back with real ways to help — that's the capability you're about to unleash on purpose.

Apply

You just used an agent. It wasn't scary. That "not scary" feeling is the whole point.

✅ You finish Day 1 having used an agent and lost the fear.
DAY2
Build the brain — your prototype agent
~15 min · you build your own, hand-held
›
Learn

Your agent lives in a saved ChatGPT conversation called a Project (or a pinned chat). You give it your instructions once; it remembers and works for you every time you open it.

Do it (free tier)
  1. Open chatgpt.com → create a New Project (name it, e.g. "My Agent").
  2. Paste this as the first message, then press send — this is your agent's operating manual:
You are my [AVENUE] agent. My superpower is [YOUR SUPER POWER / what I'm great at]. Work from my instructions, keep replies short and practical, ask me one question at a time, and never invent facts. First, ask me what I do and the one problem to start with.
  1. Upload one real file (a list, a draft, a customer note) so it knows your business.
  2. Test it on one real example. It performs the workflow instantly.
Free vs Plus

This works on free (a Project = your agent). If you have Plus/Pro, you can go further: save it as a custom GPT, or schedule it to run automatically.

Apply

DAY3
Connect your real tools (the unlock)
~20 min · let it act in Gmail, Sheets, Slack, Outlook, Monday
›
Learn

Free ChatGPT is your brain — it writes brilliantly. To make it act in the tools you already use (send the email, write the sheet row, ping Slack), we connect it through a free connector. Don't want to connect anything yet? That's fine — everything still works; you just copy the output.

Do it — the one shared connector guide
  1. Let your ChatGPT agent do the writing first — it drafts your follow-ups / messages / rows (that's the brain).
  2. Put its finished messages into a column of a Google Sheet.
  3. Create a 2-step Zap: Trigger = "New row in Google Sheets" → Action = "Gmail: send" (or "Slack: post"). It delivers whatever your agent wrote.
  4. Test once. Turn the Zap on.

Why two steps? Zapier's free plan is 2-step Zaps only (one trigger + one action) — so ChatGPT does the thinking and Zapier does the sending. Full AI-inside-the-Zap (it reads → writes → sends on its own) needs paid Zapier. That's exactly where free ends, and we're honest about it.

No-connector fallback (works for everyone): run your agent in ChatGPT, copy its output, and paste it into your tools. 100% functional — the connector just saves you the copy.

DAY4
Test a real workflow + deploy it
~15 min · run it on the real thing, turn it on
›
Learn

An agent is only as good as its last test. Today you run it on one real item, sharpen it, and make it a habit.

Do it
  1. Give your agent ONE real task: a real customer to follow up, a real overdue invoice, a real idea, a real messy inquiry.
  2. Check the output: is it human? Is it "you"? Tell it "make it warmer / shorter / less salesy" until it sounds like you.
  3. Pin the chat. Set a small routine — every morning, open it first.
